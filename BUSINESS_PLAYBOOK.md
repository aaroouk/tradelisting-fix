# TradeListing Fix launch playbook

## The offer

Sell a 24-hour Google Business Profile copy tune-up to one narrow group at a time: plumbers first, then electricians, roofers and landscapers. Deliver a profile review, rewritten description, up to 10 service descriptions, 5 FAQs and 4 Google posts. Founding price: £79 for the first five customers, then £149.

This is not a guarantee of rankings or leads. It is a clear copy and profile-completeness service.

## £50 budget

- £0: publish the included landing page on free hosting.
- £0: Google Sheets for prospects and delivery tracking.
- £0: Canva free for a clean PDF handoff.
- £0: Stripe or PayPal payment link; transaction fees only after a sale.
- £10–£15: optional domain only after the page and payment link work.
- Keep at least £35 untouched. Do not buy ads, courses, logos or subscriptions.

## Before outreach

1. Confirm the customer email in `index.html` is one you can receive today.
2. Create a Stripe or PayPal payment link for £79 and replace the email button when ready.
3. Make a six-question intake form: business name, profile URL, services, service area, ideal job, tone.
4. Make one sample audit using a real business, clearly labelled as an unsolicited sample.

## Prospecting routine

Use Google Maps manually. Search `plumber in [nearby town]`. Choose independent businesses with a genuine profile but weak descriptions, missing services or no recent posts. Do not contact businesses with obviously strong profiles. Record the business, owner/contact, one specific issue and contact date.

Send 20 genuinely personalised messages per weekday. Never mass spam. Stop contacting anyone who asks.

### First message

Subject: 3 quick fixes for [Business Name]'s Google profile

Hi [Name] — I found [Business Name] while looking at plumbers in [Town]. You have strong reviews, but your Google profile doesn't clearly mention [specific service/area], and there haven't been recent posts helping customers choose you.

I drafted three specific improvements for you. Happy to send them over free—no call or obligation. If you like them, I offer a complete 24-hour profile tune-up for a £79 founding price.

Should I send the three fixes?

— [Your name]

### Follow-up after two working days

Hi [Name] — just checking whether you'd like the three free profile fixes I noted for [Business Name]. If it isn't useful, no worries and I won't chase again.

## Free mini-audit template

1. **What I noticed:** one precise, observable weakness.
2. **Why it matters:** explain the customer confusion, without promising rankings.
3. **Suggested rewrite:** provide one useful example they can keep.
4. **Paid next step:** “I can complete the description, services, FAQs and four posts within 24 hours for £79.”

## Fulfilment checklist

1. Check the client's website and Google profile facts. Never invent credentials, locations, guarantees or services.
2. Draft all copy in one document with labelled paste locations.
3. Proofread names, phone numbers, service areas and UK spelling.
4. Deliver within the promised window with a short paste guide.
5. Ask for one round of factual corrections, a testimonial and one referral.

## Targets and reality check

Aim for 100 personalised contacts in week one, 5–15 replies, 2–5 useful conversations and one founding sale. These are operating targets, not promises. If no one engages after 100 well-targeted messages, narrow the niche or strengthen the free sample before spending money.

One £79 sale leaves roughly £75 before tax after typical payment-processing fees and recovers the entire starting budget. Profit is not guaranteed; execution, offer quality and demand determine results.
